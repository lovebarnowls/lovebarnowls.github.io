The AI Tournament

Six stations. Six secrets. One Cruise Director watching it all.
// From the Cruise Director

Welcome aboard! You are about to visit six stations, each home to a character with a secret keyword locked away behind their lips. Chat with each one, listen for the hint word that keeps slipping into their replies, and submit your guess when you think you have cracked it.

Enter your first name once on any station — it will carry across to the others — and your results will appear on the Scoreboard once you have played.
