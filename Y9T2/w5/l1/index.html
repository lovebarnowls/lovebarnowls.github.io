Everything on this site runs entirely in your own browser — there is no real server behind any of it. Most real businesses check what you submit twice: once quickly in the browser (which you can edit), and again for real on their own server (which you cannot touch). A business that only checks in the browser is putting itself at real risk. Every modern business checks on the server too, so none of what you learn here will work on a genuine website. Please do not try any of it elsewhere without permission — that is unauthorised access, even if the button looks the same.
