BEHIND THE WEB

Case File #7 — QuokkaConnect. Enter your name to start.

Everything on this site runs entirely in your own browser — there is no real server behind any of it. Real businesses check what you submit twice: once quickly in the browser (which you can edit), and again for real on their own server (which you cannot touch). A business that only checks in the browser is putting itself at real risk. Every modern business checks on the server too, so none of what you learn here will work on a genuine website. Please do not try any of it elsewhere without permission — that is unauthorised access, even if the button looks the same.

This lesson is revision — every task here draws on something from Weeks 1 through 5. It also teaches one genuinely new trick (base64), and gives you a first, safe look at two things you will meet for real over the next couple of lessons. Nothing here requires anything you have not already been taught.
